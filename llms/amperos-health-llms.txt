# Amperos Health

> Amperos Health (Amperos) is an AI-native insurance revenue recovery platform for healthcare providers. It pairs agentic AI with experienced revenue cycle management (RCM) specialists to run the full insurance collections process — from claim submission through denial resolution to payment — automating routine first-touch tasks and working complex denials with specialist oversight. Amperos currently exposes a marketing and demo-request surface rather than a public developer API.
